# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Health-conscious customers and families who want convenient meal options without planning and cooking every day. Fitness, diet, and child-focused offerings support additional customer needs.

## Product Purpose

UNAVE helps customers understand its meal offerings and plans, then enquire or place an order.

## Positioning

UNAVE is a cloud kitchen that makes healthy meal plans in its own kitchen. Customers can browse and message without an account; an account is needed only to buy a plan.

## Operating Context

- The public website presents meal offerings and plans, and supports enquiries, messages, and online purchases.
- Staff use the Payload admin to manage site content, products, enquiries, and orders.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Customers can browse meal products and plans, contact the kitchen, and purchase plans online.
- Do not promise health or medical outcomes.
- Place the required notice near medical, allergy, diabetes, and child-nutrition claims.
- Do not invent delivery areas, nutrition facts, or customer proof. Confirm these before publishing them.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is UNAVE. Existing brand guidance is maintained in `docs/design/design.md`; this file does not redefine its visual system.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing site copy and CMS-managed product content.
- Image assets in `public/media/` and `public/images/`, and brand assets in `public/brand/`. Verify provenance and suitability before treating any asset as customer or nutrition proof.

## Product Principles

1. Make meal options and plan details clear before asking customers to enquire or buy.
2. Keep browsing and messaging available without an account.
3. Be honest about nutrition, medical claims, delivery, and outcomes.
4. Reduce the daily burden of planning and cooking meals.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Meet WCAG 2.2 AA, provide interactive targets of at least 44px, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Keep the required notices near medical, allergy, diabetes, and child-nutrition claims.
